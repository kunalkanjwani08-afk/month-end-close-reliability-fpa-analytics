## 2. How to read it

- **Amounts** are USD, scale-adjusted (provisional), with signs as recorded. Costs are shown as positive magnitudes.
- **Variance** = Actual − Budget. **Variance %** = Variance ÷ |net budget|, hidden when |net budget| is under 50% of gross budget. Direction columns state arithmetic only; nothing is labelled favourable or unfavourable.
- **Primary basis** is same-sign lines; the all-lines view is always shown beside it. Opposite-sign and zero-involved lines are bridged, never removed or flipped.
- **Population:** the 58,340 Posted-only eligible P&L lines unless a table says otherwise.
- **Colour legend** (printed on Start Here): blue text is an input from Phase 4 or an adjustable assumption; black is a same-sheet formula; green links to another sheet; red would be an external link (none exist); negative numbers are red in parentheses; amber fill marks an adjustable threshold or a highlighted result.
- Tables have filters. Every sheet has a link back to Start Here.

## 4. Key formulas and logic

- Section totals on **Monthly P&L** are inputs from Phase 4 (blue); gross profit, result proxy, margins and growth are same-sheet formulas (black).
- **Result proxy** = Revenue − (COGS + People + Opex). It is a project-defined proxy, not EBITDA.
- **Gross margin %** = Gross profit ÷ Revenue, under the stated treatment of the discount account (GL 4190).
- **Variance** and **variance %** follow the rule in section 2. WAPE and bias are computed from summed absolute and signed errors, not averaged per line.
- **Reconciliation sheet** compares each workbook cell to an expected value taken from the Phase 4 analytical views, with tolerances of 0 for counts, 0.5 for USD and 1e-6 for ratios.

Formula definitions are in [FINANCIAL_METRICS_AND_METHODOLOGY.md](FINANCIAL_METRICS_AND_METHODOLOGY.md).

## 5. Using the workbook

1. Open **Start Here** and confirm the reconciliation status reads that all checks pass.
2. Read the provisional-status banner and the reading rules.
3. Use **Executive Summary** for headline figures; each is a link to its detail sheet.
4. Filter tables with the Basis or Section columns rather than editing cells.
5. If you change an adjustable input (amber cell), re-check **Reconciliation & QA**; checks tied to the original input are expected to fail.
6. Do not paste values over formulas; the checks depend on the links.

## 7. What was not verified

- **Microsoft Excel was not used.** Excel-specific behaviour (table styles, conditional-format rendering, fonts, chart appearance) has not been observed.
- Opening on screen at 100% zoom; clicking the navigation links; frozen panes, filters and conditional-format colours were checked structurally by tests only, not in a spreadsheet application.
- Tests ran on Linux, not on Windows.
- Insight text is taken from the Phase 4 register and was not re-validated by a second analyst.

## 8. Limitations

- All figures are provisional: Posted-only status, scale restoration, FX direction and the variance-% convention are working conventions; the sign convention, duplicates and consolidation are unresolved.
- Not built because the data cannot support it: EBITDA, close duration or on-time close, cost-centre variance, forecast skill or bias correction, price-volume-mix and unit economics, balance-sheet ratios, consolidated group results.
- The workbook describes the Posted-only population. Non-Posted lines appear in Data Quality as exposure, not in the P&L sheets.
